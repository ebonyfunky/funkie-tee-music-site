About

The heart behind the songs

Funkie Tee is the name I release music under. Behind every song is a real songwriter. I have been singing in choirs since I was six, and I write and produce each song myself.

The voice you hear is AI. I chose it with care, so that a full life never keeps a song from the people it was written for. The words are not generated. They are mine, written out of my own walk with God.

I believe that God is very faithful and never leaves His children helpless. Every song here is a song of hope, faith and total dependence on God.

These songs are for the one who is still waiting, the one who is tired but still trusting, and the family that wants to sing the Word together. That is why I also write Scripture songs for children: one Bible verse, one melody, made for home, Sunday school and children's choirs.

My prayer is simple: that you finish each song more sure of God than when you pressed play.

"God cannot leave me stranded."

Released through Charlen Legacy Records.
