About

I write the songs. AI sings them. God anoints and blesses.

I have been singing in choirs since I was six. I am a songwriter, and I write and produce every song here myself. AI helps me turn them into finished music, and the voice you hear is AI.

I made that choice with care. I am a healthcare professional and a serial entrepreneur with leadership responsibilities, and my days are full. I did not want a full calendar to keep these songs from the people they were written for. AI lets me keep ministering. The tool is new. The message is not.

I believe that God is very faithful and never leaves His children helpless. Every song here is a song of hope, faith and total dependence on God.

I also compose Scripture songs for children: one Bible verse, one melody, made to be sung together at home, in Sunday school and in children's choirs.

Off the record, I am also known as Dr. Funkie Tee, a mom of 2 amazing boys who rock my world, and a healthcare professional with over 2 decades of combined experience in pharmacy, public health and clinical research.

"God cannot leave me stranded."
